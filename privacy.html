1. Who we are

HaulSync is a Windows desktop application for preparing and filing GST returns. It is operated by Mohammad Yasir Najar, a sole proprietor trading as HaulSync, Anantnag, Jammu & Kashmir, India (“we”, “us”).

For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described in this policy.

Privacy contact: haulsync2026@gmail.com
Grievance officer: Mohammad Yasir Najar

2. The short version

Your clients' data never leaves your computer. Invoices, GSTINs, bank statements, exports and backups stay in your own folders. We have no server that receives them and no ability to read them.

We only receive what is needed to sell and licence the software: your name, email, phone, billing details, a hashed machine identifier, your computer name, the app version, and the date and IP address of licence checks.

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

3. Data that stays on your computer

The following is created and stored only on your PC, under %LOCALAPPDATA%\HaulSync\ or the backup location you choose:

  • client records, GSTINs, trade names and recipient masters
  • invoice, credit/debit note and e-way bill data
  • uploaded bank statements and reconciliation records
  • generated GSTR-1 workbooks, JSON files and filing packs
  • automatic and manual backups
  • operator profile details used on filings

This data is not transmitted to us. Because we never receive it, you remain its controller, and this policy does not apply to it — your own obligations under the DPDP Act continue to govern it.

Aadhaar numbers, PANs and phone numbers are never stored in your client database. Where such an identifier is used as a correction key, only a one-way SHA-256 hash is kept.

5. Data we do not collect

  • contents of your invoices, bank statements or GST filings
  • GSTINs of your clients
  • Aadhaar or PAN numbers
  • passwords for any government portal — HaulSync never asks for them
  • location data, contacts, or files outside the folders you select
  • analytics, advertising identifiers or behavioural profiles

6. Cookies and tracking

The public website sets no cookies for visitors and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics. A session cookie is created only when an administrator signs in to the private administration area.

Our hosting providers — GitHub (Pages and Releases) and Tailscale (store tunnel) — keep standard server logs such as IP address and timestamp under their own privacy policies.

8. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as far as needed:

  • Google (Gmail SMTP) — delivery of verification codes, invoices and licence emails
  • Tailscale — publishes the licence store
  • GitHub — hosts the download page, releases and this policy
  • Your bank or UPI provider — processes your payment; we never see your credentials
  • Professional advisers or authorities — where required by law, or to establish or defend a legal claim

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not use it for profiling or automated decision-making.

Some providers process data outside India. Where that happens, we rely on their contractual data-protection commitments and transfer only the minimum necessary.

10. How we protect it

  • all traffic between the app and the licence service uses HTTPS
  • licence keys are stored as hashes, with an encrypted copy kept only so we can resend your key if you ask for it; one-time codes are stored hashed and expire
  • the licence store and its database run on a single controlled machine, not a shared host
  • access to the administration area requires a password-protected session and is logged
  • your client data is protected by your own Windows account, and by whichever backup location you choose

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as required by the DPDP Act.

11. Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct anything inaccurate
  • erase your data, where we are not required to keep it by law
  • withdraw consent to marketing at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any email or by writing to us
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity
  • complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India

Write to haulsync2026@gmail.com from the email address on your licence. We respond within 30 days. Erasure requests are actioned within 30 days, except for records we must retain under GST or accounting law, which are deleted when that period ends.

12. Children

HaulSync is a professional accounting tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page, change the version and date above, and — for material changes — notify licence holders by email or in the app before the change takes effect. Earlier versions are available on request.
