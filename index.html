Questions

Before you install

Does one license cover all my clients?

Yes. HaulSync is licensed per user, not per client. One annual or monthly license covers unlimited GTA clients on one computer.

Can I move HaulSync to a new computer?

Yes. Deactivate the license on the old computer from the License dialog, then activate the same key on the new one.

What happens when the license expires?

HaulSync switches to read-only. You can still open the app, take backups, restore and export. Activate a renewal key to unlock editing again.

Does it work without internet?

HaulSync checks with the license server every 7 days and keeps working for 14 days offline. Activation and renewal need internet.

How do I pay?

Place an order on the store, pay by UPI or bank transfer, and submit the transaction reference. The license key is issued after the payment is verified.

Where are my clients' files stored?

On your computer, under %LOCALAPPDATA%\HaulSync\data\clients. Each client has a separate folder and database. HaulSync does not upload client data.
